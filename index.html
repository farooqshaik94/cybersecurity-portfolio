About Me

I'm Farooq Shaik, an IT Support professional based in the UAE, currently transitioning into Cybersecurity and SOC Analyst roles. My background is in end-user IT support and enterprise operations — troubleshooting hardware and software, administering Windows environments, managing IT tickets, and supporting network and infrastructure issues on the ground. That day-to-day exposure to real systems, real users, and real incidents is what pulled me toward security.

Over the past period I've been building cybersecurity fundamentals through structured learning and hands-on practice: Linux administration, networking concepts, security operations, incident response workflows, log analysis, SIEM concepts, and Python for security automation. I'm not presenting myself as someone with professional SOC experience — I'm presenting myself as someone with a solid IT operations foundation who is deliberately building toward a SOC Analyst / Cybersecurity Analyst role, one lab and one log file at a time.
